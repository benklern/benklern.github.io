About

I'm a research scientist at Smart Information Flow Technologies (SIFT), where I've worked on DARPA-funded research programs since 2020. My work sits where three fields rarely meet: language models and how we evaluate them, machine learning on wearable physiological data, and multi-agent systems that do real work.

Lately that has meant leading the engineering behind CHERI-PIT, a multi-agent system that ports real-world C/C++ to memory-safe CHERI hardware with minimal human intervention. It has also meant pretraining transformer encoders on wearable signals to track stress in six-person crews on month-long analogue missions. Earlier, I defined Recognizing Value Resonance as a new NLP task and used it in one of the earliest evaluations of moral value pluralism in LLMs. What ties it together is turning open questions into well-posed tasks, then building the systems that answer them on real data.

When I'm not working, I'm prototyping mechanical puzzles on my 3D printer, or having remarkably one-sided conversations with my dog Alfred, mostly about tennis balls.
